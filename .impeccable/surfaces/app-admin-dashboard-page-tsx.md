## Direction contract

THESIS: The admin dashboard is an academic publishing desk, not a generic analytics template. It makes course setup and semester publication direct, legible, and auditable.

OWN-WORLD: UEMP university red frames navigation and section markers; forest green is reserved for committed actions and healthy status. The pale green-gray workspace, white bordered surfaces, compact rectangular controls, Manrope typography, and authored line icons extend the student portal’s operational language.

STORY: An administrator confirms the active semester, reads institution counts and recent offering activity, creates a department-owned catalogue course, then publishes that course to a programme, level, and lecturer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A fixed desktop sidebar and compact top bar frame the operational heading, registration status, four institution facts, and recent course offerings. Course and offering forms follow immediately below. Mobile turns navigation into a dismissible drawer and stacks every control without horizontal overflow.

FORM: This is an Operate-mode extension of the approved UEMP dashboard system. Flat bordered panels, restrained density, explicit labels, dependent programme and lecturer choices, and immediate success or error feedback prioritize administrative accuracy. seed: b0b222f11ad5d4a7
