## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is a calm academic command center: identity, current semester, registrations, and academic standing are visible at a glance. It rejects the noisy admin-template habit of filling every gap with speculative widgets.

OWN-WORLD: UEMP university red anchors the navigation and section markers; forest green communicates available actions and healthy status. White work surfaces, pale green-gray canvas, hairline dividers, compact rectangular controls, Manrope typography, and authored line icons extend the established system into an operational interface.

STORY: A signed-in student immediately knows where they are, which semester is active, how many units are registered, and whether approved academic results exist. From there they can move toward course registration, registered courses, results, history, profile, or support.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A fixed desktop sidebar and compact top bar frame the workspace. The main column opens with a personal welcome and semester context, followed by three summary facts, a registration-status panel, and recent registered courses. A narrow right rail carries academic standing and quick links. Mobile replaces the sidebar with a dismissible drawer and stacks facts before course content.

FORM: The user-supplied university dashboard reference fixes the information hierarchy and dense desktop shell; it is translated into UEMP’s red/green visual language and limited to data the backend actually owns. The signature interaction is an instant, accessible mobile navigation drawer with restrained opacity and transform feedback. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
